## Company in one line

Brightloop is scheduling and dispatch software for installer and field-service companies with 5 to 50 technicians: heat pump installers, plumbers, electricians. Planners use it to build the week in 20 minutes instead of a morning, and technicians get their jobs, addresses and parts lists on their phone.

Website: https://brightloop.example

Where we are: founded 2023 in Rotterdam. 140 paying customers in the Netherlands and Belgium, first 6 in Germany. Team of 9. Raised a €1.6M seed round in September 2026, led by Harbourline Ventures.

## Goals

What we want from social is steady presence. When an installer hears about us from another installer and looks us up, they should find a company that posts every week, ships things and has customers saying good things. Nobody here is trying to go viral.

### Business goals (2026-2027)

1. Grow from 140 to 300 paying customers by end of 2027.
2. Get 40 paying customers in Germany.
3. Hire 4 people (2 engineering, 1 customer success, 1 sales for Germany).

### What social media does for those goals

1. Prospects who look us up see an active company with real customers.
2. Jonas and Maya get warm conversations from people who've seen their posts, so sales calls start further along.
3. The seed round story brings in candidates and gives investors in the next round something to read.

## Content pillars

| Pillar | What goes in it | Target mix |
|---|---|---|
| Customer proof | Trustpilot reviews, quotes from customer calls (with permission), short customer stories | 30% |
| Product | Feature launches, small improvements, 20-second how-tos | 20% |
| Planning know-how | Data from our customers' schedules (anonymised), tips for planners, opinions on how the trade runs | 25% |
| Company and founders | The seed round, hiring, the podcast, trade fairs, the team | 15% |
| News | Reactions to news about installers, heat pumps, subsidies, the labour shortage, and for Maya, startup funding | 10% |

## Post types we use

| Type | Input you give Claude | Output | Best on |
|---|---|---|---|
| News reaction | Screenshot or link of a headline | Screenshot + what it means for installers, with one of our own numbers if we have one | Brightloop LinkedIn, Brightloop X, Maya X |
| Podcast clip | Episode link, transcript, list of clips with timestamps | One post per clip, spread over about 3 months | All |
| Customer review | Trustpilot screenshot + name of the person and whether they agreed to be named | Screenshot + one or two lines of context | Brightloop LinkedIn (does best here), Instagram as a quote card |
| Feature launch | Changelog entry + screenshot or screen recording | One post per channel, angle adapted per audience | All except Maya X |

| Founder post | A story, a lesson, something that happened this week | Text post in Maya's or Jonas's voice | Maya LinkedIn, Jonas LinkedIn |
| Essay | A topic and notes | 600 to 1,200 word post | Brightloop Substack |

## Global rules

### Approval

- Claude writes every post as `To approve`, or `Needs media` if the channel needs an image or video and none is attached.
- Company channels (Brightloop LinkedIn, Instagram, X, Substack): Maya approves.
- Maya's channels: Maya approves. She has opted in to auto-approval on Maya X only, so Claude may set those to `Approved` directly.
- Jonas's channel: Jonas approves every post himself. No auto-approval, ever. If he rejects a post, Claude reads his reason in the Source field and doesn't schedule that angle for him again.

### Words and phrases we don't use

- "Excited to announce", "thrilled", "humbled", "proud to share"
- "Game-changer", "revolutionize", "next-level", "unlock", "supercharge", "seamless", "effortless"
- "In today's fast-paced world", "now more than ever", "let that sink in"
- "Here's the thing", "here's why", "the truth is", "nobody talks about"
- Delve, leverage, synergy, robust, cutting-edge, landscape, journey, empower
- "Digital transformation". Installers hear it from every salesperson and it means nothing to them.
- "Field service management solution". Say what it does.
- Engagement bait: "Agree?", "Thoughts?", "Comment YES", "Tag a planner who..."

### Links

- At most one link per post, at the end. Substack is the exception.
- Links to brightloop.example carry `?utm_source=<platform>&utm_medium=social&utm_campaign=<Campaign>`, with the Campaign field in lowercase and dashes.
- Plain URLs, no shorteners.
- Instagram: no links in captions. Say "link in bio".

### Hashtags

- LinkedIn: 0 to 2, only if a real community uses them (#warmtepomp, #installatietechniek).
- X: none.
- Instagram: 3 to 5 at the end of the caption.

### Emoji

- None on LinkedIn, X or Substack.
- Instagram: one at most, and only if it carries meaning.

### Languages

- English by default.
- Jonas posts in Dutch about half the time, for Dutch installers. He checks his own Dutch.
- German posts come later, when we have a German salesperson to approve them.

### Things we never post

- Customer names or company names without their permission. Note the permission in the Source field.
- Revenue, MRR or prices for specific customers.
- Anything about competitors by name.
- Photos of technicians or customers' homes without permission.
- Exact funding terms beyond what's in the Substack round post (amount, lead, pre-money).

## Cadence summary

- About 18 posts per week across all channels.
- No channel gets more than one post per day.
- One piece of content gets spread over weeks. A podcast episode feeds clips for about 3 months, one or two clips per week across all channels.
- On an episode release day or a launch day, the same news can go out on every channel. Any other time, stagger the same content by at least a day between channels and rewrite it for each one.
- Before scheduling, check the last 3 weeks of each channel against the content mix. If one pillar is above its target by more than 10 points, push the new post to a later week or pick another pillar.
- Quiet periods: 21 December to 4 January (one post per channel per week, nothing launch-related). Installer trade fair weeks belong to Jonas and event photos.
