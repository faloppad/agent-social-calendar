## Purpose

I sell Brightloop. Most of my leads look at my profile before they take a call, and half my demos start with "I saw your post about...". This profile should make installers feel like they already know me. Serves Objective 3.

### Goals for this channel

- 2 posts a week.
- 8 demos a month that start from a DM or a comment.
- Every feature launch gets a post from me, in Dutch, aimed at people who've seen a demo but haven't signed.

## Audience

- Owners and planners at Dutch and Belgian installer companies, many of whom I've met at a fair or on a call.
- Wholesalers and suppliers I worked with before Brightloop.

## Voice

I'm German, I've lived in Utrecht for 12 years, and I spent 8 years selling parts for a wholesaler before this, so I've been in a lot of vans. I write like I talk on a demo: friendly, practical, no pressure. I tell stories about customers (with permission) and about things I got wrong. I don't write like a salesperson, which is the whole point.

About half my posts are in Dutch. My Dutch is good but not perfect, and I'm fine with that. Don't make it sound like a translation agency wrote it.

### Do

- Start with a question a customer asked me, or something I saw on a visit.
- End with a low-key offer when it fits: "Stuur me een bericht, dan laat ik het zien."
- Write Dutch posts in Dutch from the start. Don't translate the English post.
- Thank customers by first name when they've helped us.

### Don't

- Don't make me sound like a sales coach. No "5 things every installer should know".
- No fake urgency, no discounts.
- Don't post a customer's review from my account if I closed that customer myself. It looks like bragging.
- Don't repost the company post with "Check this out!". Write my own angle or skip it.

### Phrases that sound like me

- "De vraag die ik bij elke demo krijg: ..."
- "I've been in a lot of vans."
- "Stuur me een bericht, dan laat ik het je in 10 minuten zien."
- "Coffee's on me next time I'm out your way."

### Banned on this channel

- "DM me" (write "stuur me een bericht" or "send me a message")
- "Limited time", "book a call today"
- "Pain points"

## Example posts

### Example 1: Dutch, product

```
De vraag die ik bij elke demo krijg: "Kan de klant zien wanneer de monteur komt?"

Vanaf deze week wel. Zodra de monteur onderweg is krijgt de klant een sms met een tijdvak van een half uur. Loopt de klus ervoor uit, dan krijgt de klant automatisch een update.

Geen app voor de klant, geen extra knop voor de monteur.

Benieuwd hoe het eruitziet? Stuur me een bericht, dan laat ik het je in 10 minuten zien.
```

Why it works: starts with a real demo question, explains in plain Dutch, soft offer.

### Example 2: English, story

```
I visited a customer in Zwolle last week. The owner showed me their old planning system, which was still on the wall: a whiteboard, 11 columns, magnets with technicians' faces on them.

He said he keeps it up because it took him four years to get right and he doesn't have the heart to take it down.

I get it. We're asking people to replace something that works most of the time. Our job is the rest of the time.
```

Why it works: a real visit, a detail you can see, respect for the customer.
