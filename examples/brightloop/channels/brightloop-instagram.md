## Purpose

Planners and younger installers use Instagram more than LinkedIn. This account shows the work side: technicians, vans, trade fairs, the team, and short product clips. It also makes us look like a real company to anyone who checks. Serves Objective 1.

### Goals for this channel

- 2 posts every week.
- Every podcast clip from episode 58 goes out here as a video.
- Get to 600 followers by end of December (from 310).

## Audience

- Planners and office staff at installer companies.
- Technicians, some of whom use the Brightloop app every day.
- Friends of the team, honestly. That's fine.

## Voice

Same "we" as LinkedIn, shorter and lighter. One idea per post. Captions read like something you'd say out loud to a colleague, not a press release.

### Do

- First line works on its own, because that's all most people see.
- Keep captions under 300 characters most of the time.
- Show people and real places when we have permission.

### Don't

- Don't put links in captions. They don't work. Say "link in bio".
- No walls of hashtags.
- No stock photos. A slightly blurry real photo beats a stock one.

### Phrases that sound like us

- "Whiteboards are great until Tuesday."
- "Link in bio."
- "Thanks for the review, Ruud."

### Banned on this channel

- Emoji as bullets. One emoji per post at most.

## Hashtags, mentions and links

- Hashtags: 3 to 5 at the end of the caption. Pick from #installers #warmtepomp #heatpumps #fieldservice #installatietechniek #planning #smallbusiness.
- Mentions: tag customers and events only with permission.
- Links: none in the caption. "Link in bio" when there's somewhere to go.

## Media rules

- Every post needs an image or video. If none is attached, Claude sets the status to `Needs media` and writes in the Source field what the image should be.
- Images 1080x1350 (4:5). Video 1080x1920 (9:16) or 1080x1350.
- Quote cards: Brightloop green #1F7A5A background, white text, max 25 words of quote.
- Captions burned into every video. Most people watch without sound.

## Example posts

### Example 1: podcast clip (video)

```
Whiteboards are great until Tuesday.

Maya on The Ground Floor podcast, on why most installers still plan by hand and what breaks first. Full episode, link in bio.

#installers #fieldservice #heatpumps #planning
```

Why it works: a first line that stands alone, and the clip does the rest.

### Example 2: trade fair photo (carousel)

```
Two days at the installer fair in Gorinchem. Jonas talked to about 80 planners. The most common question: "Can my technicians use it without calling me?" Yes.

#installatietechniek #warmtepomp #installers
```

Why it works: real place, a real question we got, short answer.

### Example 3: review quote card

```
Monday mornings at Dekker Installatietechniek used to mean rebuilding the week on a whiteboard. Now it takes 20 minutes.

Thanks for the review, Ruud.

#installers #heatpumps #fieldservice
```

Why it works: the card carries the quote, the caption just thanks him.
