## Purpose

Few installers read X. This account exists so that launches and news reactions have a public, linkable home, and so the startup and investor crowd that follows Maya can find the company. Low effort, kept steady. Serves Objective 1.

### Goals for this channel

- 3 posts every week.
- Every feature launch and every news reaction gets a post here.

## Audience

- Other founders, investors, journalists who cover startups.
- A handful of tech-minded installers.

## Voice

"We", short, factual. One point per post. Reads like a changelog written by someone with opinions.

### Do

- Lead with the fact.
- Use numbers.
- Write it so it reads fine without the link.

### Don't

- No threads for launches. One post, one link.
- No hashtags.
- Don't reply-guy other companies' posts from this account.

### Phrases that sound like us

- "New: ..."
- "In our customers' data, ..."
- "On for everyone today."

### Banned on this channel

- Hashtags
- "Big news", "huge update"

## Formats and length

## Hashtags, mentions and links

- Hashtags: none.
- Mentions: @ the podcast or event account when there is one. Tag Maya on her clips.
- Links: one at most, with UTM (`utm_source=x`).

## Media rules

- Images optional. Launch posts get a screenshot or a short screen recording.
- Images 1200x675 (16:9).

## Example posts

### Example 1: launch

```
New: arrival texts. Customers get a 30-minute window when the technician leaves for the job, and an update if the job before runs over. On for everyone today.
```

Why it works: what it is, what it does, when. Done.

### Example 2: news reaction

```
6 to 9 weeks for a heat pump install in NL right now. In our customers' data most of that wait is survey visits, parts and second trips, not the install itself.
```

Why it works: their headline, our number, one sentence.

## Performance notes

- 2026-10-19 | Average 2 engagements per post at 4 per week. Launches and news reactions do 3 to 5x better than filler tips | Cut to 3 per week. Launches and news first, know-how only to fill gaps.
