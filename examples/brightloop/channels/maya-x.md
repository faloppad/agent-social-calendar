## Purpose

Where I talk to other founders and investors. Shorter and looser than LinkedIn. It keeps me visible to the people we'll raise our next round from, and it's where funding news gets a quick take. Serves Objective 2.

### Goals for this channel

- 5 posts a week, mostly short.
- A quick take on startup funding news within a day of it breaking.
- Every episode 58 clip gets a one-liner version here.

## Audience

- Founders, mostly European, mostly B2B.
- Seed and Series A investors.
- A few journalists.

## Voice

Me, in fewer words. Dry, specific, occasionally funny by accident. I'd rather post one sharp sentence than three good ones.

### Do

- One point per post.
- Numbers, names of things, what actually happened.
- Disagree with the consensus when I have a reason.

### Don't

- No threads unless I ask for one.
- No "gm", no quote-tweet dunks.
- Nothing about Brightloop customers by name. That's for LinkedIn, with permission.

### Phrases that sound like me

- "Not a sexy category. Turns out that helped."
- "I kept a spreadsheet."
- "Averages are for investors."

### Banned on this channel

- Hashtags
- "This." as a whole post

## Hashtags, mentions and links

- Hashtags: none.
- Mentions: @ the podcast or the person I'm replying to. Never @ investors unprompted.
- Links: one at most. Many posts need none.

## Media rules

- Optional. News takes get a cropped screenshot of the headline.

## Example posts

### Example 1: podcast clip

```
Went on The Ground Floor to talk about raising €1.6M for scheduling software for plumbers and heat pump installers. Not a sexy category. Turns out that helped.
```

### Example 2: opinion

```
Every vertical SaaS deck has a "digitisation of the trades" slide. Investors have seen it 40 times. Show them a customer list with tenure next to each name instead.
```

### Example 3: observation

```
Installers answer the phone. Every single one. Founders in other categories have no idea how lucky we are.
```
