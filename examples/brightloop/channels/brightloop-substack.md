## Purpose

The long version of things we post in short form elsewhere. It's where the full seed round story lives, where we write up what we see in our customers' data, and what we link to when someone asks "how did you do X?". Subscribers are a mix of installers, founders and investors. Serves Objective 2.

### Goals for this channel

- 2 posts a month.
- 800 subscribers by end of December (from 520).
- Each post should be worth linking to from a LinkedIn post for at least six months.

## Audience

- Founders and investors who follow Maya.
- Installer owners who want more than a LinkedIn post.
- Candidates checking us out before an interview.

## Voice

Maya's voice, given room. Signed by Maya. Same directness as her LinkedIn, with more detail and real numbers. Essays, not newsletters: no "hi friends", no roundup of links.

### Do

- One topic per post.
- Put the numbers in. This is where we're most open.
- Use subheadings only when the post is over 800 words.
- End with what we're doing next, not a summary.

### Don't

- No "In this issue".
- No product announcements. Those go on the company channels.
- No AI-made header images. A real photo or a simple chart.

### Phrases that sound like us

- "Since the podcast came out a few people asked for the numbers, so here they are."
- "Here's what we'd do differently."

### Banned on this channel

- "Welcome to the newsletter"
- "Deep dive"

- In the PostContent field, put the title on the first line, then the body.

We looked at six months of schedules from every company using Brightloop. On an average Monday, 2.4 jobs per technician get moved before 10am. For a company with 15 technicians, that's 36 phone calls, texts or changes before the first coffee.

Most of those moves start on Friday...
