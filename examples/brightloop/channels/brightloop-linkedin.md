## Purpose

This is the page installers land on after someone mentions us. It has one job: show that we're active, we ship, and customers like us. Serves Objective 1 directly and Objective 2 through podcast clips.

### Goals for this channel

- 3 posts every week. No gap longer than 7 days.
- At least one customer review or customer story every two weeks.
- Followers 1,900 → 2,500 by end of December.

## Audience

- Owners of installer companies who heard about us from another installer and are checking us out.
- Planners at existing customers, who share our posts in their own company chat more often than they like them.
- A few investors and candidates.

## Voice

"We", as a team. Plain and practical, like a good planner explaining something to a new colleague. We know the trade, so we use its words (survey visit, callback, parts run) and skip software words. Warm about customers, dry about ourselves.

### Do

- Open with the specific thing: the customer, the number, the problem.
- Name the trade. "Heat pump installers" beats "field-service businesses".
- Keep paragraphs to one or two sentences.
- When we have one of our own numbers, use it. Say it comes from our customers' schedules.
- End when the point is made. A closing line that restates the post is one line too many.

### Don't

- Don't explain what Brightloop is in every post. The page header does that.
- No "we're proud to" or "we're happy to". Just say what happened.
- No questions at the end to farm comments.
- Don't make installers sound behind or old-fashioned. Whiteboards exist for good reasons.

### Phrases that sound like us

- "That's the bit we built Brightloop for."
- "Monday mornings are the reason we started this company."
- "From our customers' schedules: ..."
- "Nothing for the technician to do."

### Banned on this channel

- "Solution", "platform" (say "Brightloop" or "the planner")
- "Users" (they're customers, planners, technicians)

## Hashtags, mentions and links

- Hashtags: 0 to 2. #warmtepomp and #installatietechniek are the only ones worth using.
- Mentions: tag a customer's company page only if they said yes. Tag Maya or Jonas when the post is about them.
- Links: one at most, at the end, with UTM (`utm_source=linkedin`).

## Media rules

- Most posts should have an image. Text-only is fine for news reactions.
- Review screenshots: crop to the review, keep the star rating, remove the browser bar.
- Product screenshots: use the demo account (Van Dijk Installaties, fake data). Never a real customer's schedule.
- Data visuals: one chart, one colour (Brightloop green #1F7A5A) on white, big labels, readable on a phone.
- Video: square or 4:5, captions burned in.

## Example posts

### Example 1: customer review

```
Ellen plans jobs for 9 technicians at Klaassen Elektro in Tilburg. She left us this review on Friday:

"I used to keep half the week in my head. When I was sick for a day nobody could figure out who was going where. Now it's all in Brightloop and my colleague just opens it."

That second sentence is the one we hear most from planners. It's rarely about speed. It's about being able to take a day off.
```

Why it works: a real person with a real job, her own words, and one line from us about why it matters.

### Example 2: data visual

```
From our customers' schedules: a heat pump install that needs a second visit takes on average 9 days longer from first call to finished job.

The most common reason for the second visit isn't the install. It's a missing part that nobody checked against the survey notes.

Chart: share of second visits by reason, last 6 months, 140 companies.
```

Why it works: one number, one reason, something a planner can act on this week.

### Example 3: product

```
Small one this week. When a technician marks a job as "needs return visit", Brightloop now asks what's missing and adds it to the parts list for the next visit.

It came from Kees at Dekker Installatietechniek, who was tired of texting his technicians to ask what they needed.
```

Why it works: small, concrete, credits the customer who asked for it.

## Performance notes

- 2026-10-19 | Review screenshots average 3x the reactions of feature posts and get shared by other installers | Keep one review every 1 to 2 weeks. Don't stack two in a row.
- 2026-10-12 | Posts that open with a customer's name and trade get read further (higher click-through on "see more") than posts that open with Brightloop | Open with the customer.
- 2026-10-05 | The one carousel we did (5 slides) got good reach but few clicks | Use carousels for walkthroughs only, not for announcements.
