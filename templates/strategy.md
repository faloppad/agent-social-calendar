### Approval

<!--
Who approves what. The default that works: company channels go to one owner;

-->

- Claude writes every post as `To approve` (or `Needs media` if the platform needs an image and none is attached).
- Company channels: [name] approves.
- Personal channels: the person approves their own posts. Always.
- Auto-approval: [none / list channels whose owner opted in to `Approved` directly].
